# Suicune park artwork

The creator requested a small north-west dock-city park, a giant stone Suicune
sculpture, candle offerings and residents seeking the land protector's help.
The monument keeps Suicune's familiar silhouette. Its weathered grey stone,
carved mane, ribbons and moss fit the existing perpetual-night coastal palette.
Small offerings give the scale a human counterpoint; local amber light uses the
existing sheltered-lamp presentation at a reduced size.

Approved sources are `content/props/suicune_monument/image.png`,
`content/props/votive_offerings/image.png` and `content/props/park_bench/image.png`.
Original generated PNGs are copied without resizing, recolouring or alpha edits.
Their prop declarations own native display scale and source-pixel anchors.
The two-row pedestal has native map tile collision; the tall sculpture does not
block walking behind its upper body.

Generated using OpenAI's built-in image tool. The statue used the supplied
Essentials Suicune front sprite as its visual reference; original Pokémon
attribution remains applicable. The offerings and bench were generated from text.
