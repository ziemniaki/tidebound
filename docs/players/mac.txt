Tidebound — Mac (Intel and Apple Silicon)

Open Tidebound.app after extraction. The app can stay in Downloads or another
folder; moving it to Applications is optional. Keep the app bundle intact.
No Python, Wine or RPG Maker installation is needed to play.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. macOS may require first-launch approval
under Privacy & Security. Do not disable system security to run it.

Controls: arrows to move; Return to interact/confirm; Esc or X for menu/back.
Fn-F1 opens key bindings on keyboards that reserve F1 for brightness.
Save through the in-game menu.

Resize the window to enlarge the whole game, including menus and text.
The picture keeps its proportions; wider or taller windows have black bars.
Whole-number scales stay crisp; intermediate sizes use light smoothing.
Options > Screen Size offers presets and Full; Option+Return toggles fullscreen.

Saves live outside the app:
~/Library/Application Support/Tidebound_Opening_0_2/
Replacing the app leaves this folder intact. Back up an existing save before
starting and saving a new game. Saves do not transfer between computers by themselves.

For a problem, report the release version, macOS version, Intel/Apple Silicon,
exact error text and steps to reproduce at github.com/ziemniaki/tidebound/issues.
Keep your save when reporting a gameplay problem.

Unofficial Pokémon fan project. CREDITS.md and the bundled runtime license/source
retain third-party attribution. Native launch checks are not a full playthrough.
