# Opening walkthrough

**Spoilers for the current playable chapters.** See [current status](status.md) for its scope
and [release notes](release-notes.md) for changes between versions.

## Controls and saves

Extract the complete player package, then open Tidebound.app on Mac, Game.exe on
Windows or Tidebound.sh on Linux. Use arrows to move, **Enter** to interact and
**Esc** for the menu or to back out. **F1** shows the launcher's key bindings.
Save from the menu; Continue resumes your existing journey.

A new journey includes 99 Rare Candies for evolution testing. Continuing does
not refill them. Back up a save before replacing it with a new journey. Developer
builds and declared playtest starts have [separate save slots](development.md#playtest-scenarios).

## The dream opening

1. Guide the sleeping Natu through the dim paths to the child's bed. The caption's
   “100 steps” is flavour here; reach the bed to continue.
2. In the first false bedroom, read the cupboard books. At the bed, answer
   **Thursday**, **The empty chair**, **Under the pillow**. Choose **12 years**
   of sleep, then speak to Wick. No real-time waiting is required.
3. In the larger bedroom, read the scattered books and reach the bed. Answer
   **Beneath the floor**, **A mouthful of snow**, **Before**, **Nobody in your voice**.
   A wrong answer returns you to that room's entrance.
4. In Wick's maze, follow arrows, stop on diamonds and use the round teleporters.
   Find Wick; Mother's call returns you to the ordinary bedroom. Go downstairs.

Turning the backwards book on the first room's shelf reveals an optional
**Water curse** leaf. It is not required to leave. The opening does not replay
on an existing journey that has already passed it.

## Pookie, the keys and the lamp

After Mother's conversation, find Pookie asleep outside the lighthouse. Walk
100 steps together around the village. Near the pier she may run away; speak
to her at its end to resume following. The pier incident is optional.

Return home together and choose Wick the Natu, Maku the Makuhita or Pookie the
Poochyena. Mother asks for lamp oil. Speak to the seller beside his locked shop,
find his keys among the white flowers in the northern forest, and return them.
Enter the shop, collect the oil and tend the great lamp upstairs at home.
A later pier visit offers another glimpse of the shore's mystery.

The notebook records the current errand and lets you rename Ren. The forest
pool is dangerous; retrieving the keys does not require a battle.

## The pie and stolen necklace

The seller gives you pie. Speak to Mother to eat together, then return the washed
plate to the shop. The robbery sends you south along the coast road. Defeat the
first thief and follow the second to the old storehouse.

Inside, navigate the gaps through the rubbish and defeat Bram near the sofa.
Speak to Ivo and accept his game. In **The Mending**, arrows move, Enter loosens
a nearby stitch and Esc leaves. Release all three nests, then reach the northern
mouth. Contractions preserve released nests; leaving before completion allows a
free retry. The minigame does not harm or alter your actual party.

After winning the game, defeat Ivo in a Pokémon battle. Follow him to the cupboard
and collect the necklace. A trainer loss does not erase your minigame win. If the
bag cannot accept the necklace, make room and speak to him again.

Return the necklace to the seller. If an existing journey already finished the
oil errand, the seller can still give you the pie; completed quests stay completed.

## The vault, museum and harbour

Collect the seller's Blue-Reed Keepsake, follow him to the lighthouse and speak
to Mother. Take the hall stairs down through the cellar to the vault. After their
conversation, take the east path beside the storehouse to the docks. Enter the
museum and inspect the sabre. A journey with the necklace already returned can
start this chapter by speaking to the seller in his shop.

Explore the two sailing ships and speak to their crews. Nell and Oren offer
optional battles; neither gates the story. Shop frontages remain closed.

At the end of the eastern pier, speak to the captain about **Psyduck Island**.
Accepting reserves passage for a future chapter. You remain free to explore and save;
the voyage and island are not yet playable.

## Optional exploration and recovery

The pond below the South Coast Road has optional fishermen battles, a visible
regional Psyduck, grass encounters and an Oran tree. Search behind the western
thicket for Mystic Water. The island obelisk needs Surf, which the current game does not
award. Species evolution and battle-role notes are in [species design](../specs/species.md).

Resting fires in the forest and on the road set a return checkpoint and restore
full HP/PP once every 15 real minutes per fire. They do not cure poison or other
status conditions. Berry trees give two berries and regrow after an hour.
Cooldowns persist in saves; ordinary paths avoid random grass encounters.

Losing the whole party in battle leads beyond the shore. A borrowed Natu helps
you fight the wandering forms of your fallen companions. Each spirit has one
encounter and a six-turn deadline. Catching it restores the original individual,
including its nickname, owner, moves and held item. Defeating the spirit, fleeing,
losing or running out of turns loses that companion.

Leaving also loses any spirits still waiting; the exit asks first. A memorial
records losses. With no survivors, a different Natu accompanies your return so
the journey can continue; the borrowed guide stays behind. Ordinary manual saves
remain available, so loading an earlier save can roll back losses.
