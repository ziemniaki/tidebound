# Whyduck — Tidebound Demo 1 / 0.8.0

Regional Psyduck: Water/Psychic; familiar Psyduck art and base stats; evolves at level 16. Wild shore encounters are rare (10% of southern road grass, levels 4–6). A fixed visible level-8 regional Psyduck also appears on South Coast Road. Ordinary Psyduck and Golduck remain in the dex and in existing saves.

Whyduck: Water/Psychic, Still Mind Pokémon. Height 1.1 m, weight 26.0 kg. Base stats HP 75 / Attack 55 / Defense 70 / Speed 75 / Sp. Attack 130 / Sp. Defense 115; total 520. Own Tempo or Cloud Nine; hidden Inner Focus. Psychic is its evolution move. Psychic line: Psybeam (9), Confusion (1), Psyshock (40), Future Sight (48), Psychic Terrain (56), Stored Power (65). Water line: Water Gun (1), Water Pulse (13), Brine (44), Hydro Pump (52). Calm Mind (35), Recover (60) and other support moves provide tactical choices.

Dex: Its headaches ceased when the folds on its head opened. It stands quietly by the tide for hours, as if considering a question it cannot ask.

Visual direction: Inspired by the supplied penguin pose, Whyduck keeps its duck bill, rounded Psyduck-yellow body, small feet and short tail. Its wings make an asymmetric incantation pose, away from its head. A visibly opened skull has a jagged bone rim, an inner shadow and broad, pink exposed brain tissue with two uneven hemispheres and winding folds. The shiny brain is green. Front, back, shiny variants and two animated icon frames are manually plotted on the native 2x Gen 3 pixel grid. The organ's relationship to any other magic remains unknown. Psyduck's cry remains a declared placeholder.

Approved 0.7.24 visual: yellow Psyduck-like body with orange flank/golden belly, pink exposed brain, original bill and eyes with pupils down-left, original hand pixels in uneven casting pose mirrored in back view. Shiny: blue body and green brain.
