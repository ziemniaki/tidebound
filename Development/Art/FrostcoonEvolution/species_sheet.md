# Nivalora - Tidebound 0.7.12

Working name: Nivalora. Ice/Dragon. Kindly Wings Pokemon.
Frostcoon evolves at level 55 on the cocoon half of regional Wurmple's fixed
personality split. The other half still becomes Glaciverm at level 10.

A rare, beautiful, benevolent butterfly dragon. Very fast special attacker with
strong special bulk and much weaker physical defence. Shield Dust blocks the
additional effects of damaging moves; it does not block direct status moves.
No hidden ability. No moves that inflict poison in its learnset/tutor/egg data.
Sleep Powder keeps native 75% accuracy and immunities. Dragon Breath can still
paralyze, and Ice Beam/Blizzard can freeze: ordinary native move effects remain.
Dragon Dance raises Attack/Speed, not Special Attack; later Quiver Dance gives
its special setup. Four move slots require choosing offence, sleep and support.

Weak to Fighting, Rock, Dragon, Steel and Fairy (2x). Resists Water, Electric
and Grass. Airborne artwork does not grant Flying typing or Levitate.

## Pokedex entry

Rarely seen, it shelters lost travelers beneath its frost-white wings. Its gentle scales lull frightened creatures to sleep, and it waits beside them until they wake.

Inherited Frostcoon moves are retained. Dragon Breath is offered on evolution;
Ice Beam is also offered when evolution occurs at 55. Above 55, native move
reminder rules apply to missed moves. Cancelling evolution or holding Everstone
works normally. No forced evolution or party replacement on loading a save.

## Machine/tutor compatibility

Protect, Endure, Substitute, Safeguard, Light Screen, Reflect, Hail, Rain Dance, Helping Hand, Ice Beam, Blizzard, Icy Wind, Dragon Pulse, Draco Meteor, Roost, Tailwind, Aerial Ace, Acrobatics.

Compatibility does not add new TM locations or tutors. No egg moves.

## Healing

Wish heals the occupant of its battle position at the end of the next turn;
a teammate can switch in to receive it. Life Dew restores 25% HP to active
allies, not the bench. Roost restores half the user's HP and leaves its Ice/Dragon
typing unchanged. These moves do not cure poison, revive the fallen, or add
field healing. No Rest, Heal Bell, Aromatherapy or Healing Wish is granted.

## Supporting data

Height 2.4 m; weight 22.0 kg; catch rate 45; base experience 300; 3 Speed EVs;
Medium growth; starting happiness 70; 50:50 gender ratio; Bug/Dragon egg groups;
3,840 hatch steps. No held-item drops, no further evolution or alternate form.
The line retains Medium growth to preserve its existing progression and saves.
It has the requested pseudo-legendary stat total; no Slow-growth retrofit.

## Art and sound

Approved white/pale-blue butterfly dragon with Wurmple's golden eyes, cream
split muzzle, head horn and curled tail. Corrected rear wing pose retained.
Front/back 160x160; two-frame party icon 128x64. Normal/shiny files registered
under NIVALORA; shiny currently shares the same palette. Reuses Articuno's cry
provisionally. No new original cry or shiny design is claimed.

## Availability and continuity

Obtain by raising the existing cocoon branch to level 55. No wild encounter is
added. Already owned Frostcoon above 55 qualify at their next level-up. Nickname,
identity, IVs/EVs, item and history follow normal evolution. Narrative rarity
does not replace the established roughly even Wurmple personality split.
